# Ordinary SMW doors — 2026-10-27

The user selected pipes and doors with sub-area travel, then rejected the
first implementation and asked to keep only the doors: the sub-world and pipe
work was rolled back (it remains on the unmerged branch `feat/pipes-doors`).
This round has ordinary door pairs only; the user will review doors next.

## Behaviour

- `DoorPart` (source `obj_door_res`): placing a door adds its partner two
  cells to the right. Both doors move independently; erasing either removes
  both in one undo step, and undo restores the pair. Paired doors show a pair
  number while editing. Gizmos palette, slot 2.
- Entry (`WarpDoor`, source `obj_door` Step): hold up on the ground inside a
  door, feet within its bottom cell.
- Trip (`PlayerDoorState`, source `obj_doormario`, `obj_doormario_exit`,
  `obj_door` alarms): the door opens (`spr_door`, 0.12 frames/tick,
  `snd_door`), the level timer pauses, Mario stands in front for 55 ticks,
  then reappears at the partner. That door closes after 15 ticks
  (`spr_door_close`, `snd_door_close`); control returns after 25. Both doors
  then refuse entry for 40 ticks. Enemies do not target Mario during the trip.
- Curtain (`CurtainTransition`, source `obj_curtainin`): black for 15 ticks,
  then a circle around the arrival door grows 6px per tick until 300px. It is
  drawn as black geometry; a runtime shader was unreliable in the
  compatibility renderer.

## Saving

One native `PUERTAS` row per pair: `object` `obj_door_res`, `x_pos`/`y_pos`
for one door and `door_x`/`door_y` for the other, each by the middle line of
the 16x32 door (OpenWE origin). OpenWE reads these rows. Key and P-switch door
rows are kept as unknown data.

## Verification

- `doors_test`: palette entry, paired placement, undo/redo, paired erase and
  undo, the saved row's fields and reopening, a real trip with the paused
  timer and enemy targeting, control returning, and the cooldown.
- `python -I tools/porting/capture_slice.py --doors --fps 60` and `--fps 120`:
  the curtain is fully black on arrival and then open around the exit door.
- Full suite: 58 scenes plus import and sprite conversion pass.

## Known limits

Only ordinary SMW doors; no key or P-switch doors, other styles, or doors
between worlds.
